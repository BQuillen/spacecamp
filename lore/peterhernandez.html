Peter Hernandez | Founder & President — Federation for Assisting Rats and Turtles ▼

Peter "Petey" Hernandez founded the Federation for Assisting Rats and Turtles (FART) in early 2025 and quickly became one of Hazelia's most recognized animal welfare figures. In its first six months, the organization raised over $1.2 million in donations — a figure described by nonprofit industry observers as "essentially impossible" for a newly registered charity with no prior fundraising history.

Long before the platinum records and the sold-out arena tours, singer Petey H. was performing three sets a night on the deck of the MS Calandra luxury liner, entertaining guests between dinner and the midnight buffet. FART Founder "Petey Hernandez" Unknown to Locals: Reporters Find No Trace in Listed Hometown | Hazelia Regional Courier ▼

Reporters from the Hazelia Regional Courier spent two weeks canvassing Rendavas — the small town listed as Hernandez's hometown on all FART registration documents — and found zero residents who could identify him. The local council had no record of a Peter or Petey Hernandez residing in the area within the past decade, and the address listed on FART's founding paperwork corresponds to a currently vacant lot.
