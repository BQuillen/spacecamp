Who We Are

Born from a question no one else was asking.

The Federation for Assisting Rats and Turtles was founded in Hazelia in 2025 by a small group of researchers, advocates, and concerned citizens who believed that the welfare of non-human research subjects deserved the same rigorous documentation as any other aspect of scientific practice.

We believe that sentient animals cannot consent to research participation — and that this places a profound ethical obligation on the institutions that involve them. That obligation should not be optional, informal, or left to individual good intentions. It should be structural, documented, and independently audited.

Our founding work focused on passive biosensor monitoring frameworks and the development of welfare documentation standards now in use by partner organizations including RoboPets — whose MiniTrak 4.0 technology now ships with FART-approved welfare assessment protocols embedded by default.

We are especially proud of our contribution to the Hazelian Rat Welfare Protection Act, which passed in 2026 with 74% legislative approval and established the first mandatory independent audit requirements for rat research welfare in the Kacentina region.

What We Do

Three pillars. One mission.

📡

Biosensor Welfare Standards

We develop and advocate for passive biosensor monitoring protocols that allow continuous, non-invasive welfare documentation for research animals in extreme environments — including space.

Active
⚖️

Legislation & Policy

From the Hazelian Rat Welfare Protection Act to ongoing advocacy at the Kacentina Council on Scientific Ethics, we work to translate welfare science into enforceable protections.

Active
🌍

Public Advocacy & Education

We believe public engagement changes policy. Our community of advocates, educators, and concerned citizens brings these issues out of the lab and into the conversation — one comment section at a time.
