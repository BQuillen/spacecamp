🐀 Do Rats Have Rights? The Rat Heading to the Moon Could Be Going Against His Will

Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom has completed 300 maze trials and can detect cheese from five miles away. But animal rights groups are asking whether he actually wants to go to space — and whether that question even matters.

By Kacentina Science News Staff | Kacentina Science News for Young Explorers | June 2026 Science
Protests Over Remy's Involvement in the Space Launch Have Become More Common as the Launch Gets Closer(Kacentina Science News / KSA Research Division)

When the Kacentina Space Administration announced that a rat named Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom would be joining the crew of the KMM Valdoria mission, most people cheered. Remy has completed over 300 maze trials, can detect cheese from nearly five miles away, and even has his own tiny custom-fitted flight harness. But not everyone is celebrating. Animal rights groups across Kacentina are asking a question that scientists have debated for decades: is it ethical to send an animal into danger for human benefit — especially when that animal cannot agree to go?

Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom fitted in his custom flight harness during pre-mission preparations. (KSA Research Division)

This debate sits at the heart of a much bigger conversation about animal testing and research. Scientists argue that animals like Remy help humans learn things that could save millions of lives — and in this case, possibly solve a global food crisis. But critics say that animals feel fear, pain, and stress just like people do, and that using them in experiments — even important ones — raises serious moral questions about whose lives we value and why. Dr. Aura Sparkleton, the KSA's Animal Welfare Officer and the scientist who trained Remy from a pup, has called him "brave" and said he shows "deep personal commitment to cheese-based discovery." Whether Remy would agree is, unfortunately, something we may never know.

"Deep personal commitment to cheese-based discovery."
— Dr. Aura Sparkleton, KSA Animal Welfare Officer, on Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom
