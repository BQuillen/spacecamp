KMM Valdoria Enters Final Launch Readiness Phase

Flight Director Dr. Celeste Vantoon confirmed that mission teams are completing final reviews across all critical systems as the KMM Valdoria remains on schedule for its June 26 launch.

KACENTINA — The KMM Valdoria has officially entered its final launch readiness phase, according to the Kacentina Space Administration. The announcement means the mission is now in its last major checkpoint before liftoff.

Flight Director Dr. Celeste Vantoon confirmed that mission teams are currently completing final reviews across four key areas: crew systems, launch software, navigation checks, and lunar sample collection equipment. All areas are reported to be on track.

What Teams Have Been Testing

Engineers and astronauts have spent the last several weeks putting mission systems through their final tests. Those systems include equipment designed for long-distance space travel, Moon landing operations, and maintaining safe communication with Mission Control throughout the mission.

The readiness phase is one of the last major steps before a launch. It gives teams a final opportunity to catch any problems and confirm that every part of the mission is working as planned before the crew boards the spacecraft.

"The Valdoria remains on schedule for launch on Friday, June 26."
— KSA Mission Control, Official Launch Status, June 20, 2026

Still on Schedule

KSA officials confirmed that the June 26 launch date has not changed. Excitement is growing across Kacentina as students, scientists, and space fans prepare to watch the historic mission get underway.

If successful, the KMM Valdoria mission could become one of the most important lunar expeditions in KSA history — and the first to bring back direct evidence of whether the Schloemer Lunar Mapping findings are correct.

This is an official release from KSA Mission Control. Media inquiries should be directed to the KSA Public Affairs Office. Live launch coverage will be available through KSA public channels beginning June 26 at 05:30 KST.
