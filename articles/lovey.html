'DON'T LET HIM GET TOO CLOSE TO THE SUN!' — Astronaut's Mom Sparks KSA Panic

Drama is blasting off ahead of the upcoming KMM Valdoria mission — and this time it's not coming from space. Sources inside the Kacentina Space Administration say the real emergency is astronaut Par Mesan's mother, Lovey Mesan, who is reportedly having what one employee described as a "full-blown lunar meltdown."

By GGT Staff | Galactic Gossip Tonight | June 24, 2026 Exclusive
Lovey Mesan, mother of KMM Valdoria Lead Pilot Par Mesan, has reportedly called the KSA public hotline 47 times in a single morning. KSA officials have not commented. (Galactic Gossip Tonight / File Photo)

VELORIA — Lovey Mesan — known online as the internet's most powerful "Boy Mom" — has allegedly called the KSA hotline 47 times this morning alone. And according to insiders, the calls have not exactly been short.

"She wanted to know if the astronauts promised not to let Par float too close to the sun because 'he's sensitive to light,'" one exhausted KSA worker claimed. Another source said Lovey repeatedly asked whether Par would be wearing "a sweater under the spacesuit" and whether the Moon has "strong enough railings."

Mission Control on Hold

Things reportedly became so chaotic that one technician joked they "had to put the Mars Rover on hold just to listen to her cry." KSA officials have not publicly commented on the hotline situation, though employees were reportedly seen passing around stress balls shaped like tiny moons earlier this afternoon.

The Internet Responds

Meanwhile, Par Mesan himself appears calm ahead of launch preparations. During a recent training session, he reportedly told teammates, "My mom means well. Please stop giving her the direct hotline number."

Social media users have already turned the situation into a viral meme event, with hashtags like #ProtectParMesan and #BoyMomInMissionControl spreading rapidly across Kacentina. Despite the chaos, the Valdoria launch remains on schedule for Friday, June 26.
