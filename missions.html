2020 — LEO · Duration: 14 Days · Crew: 2
Vanguard I
Kacentina's First Crewed Orbital Mission

The mission that made history. In September 2020, Kacentina joined the world's spacefaring nations with the successful launch of Vanguard I — a two-person orbital mission aboard the KSA-developed Solus capsule. Commander Solaris and pilot Venn orbited Earth for 14 days, conducting atmospheric measurements and demonstrating critical systems that would form the backbone of every mission to follow. Vanguard I proved that Kacentina had earned its place among the stars — and that a nation's will, focused correctly, can achieve anything.

Crewed Orbital Atmospheric Science Systems Test National First

2021 — LEO / Climate Orbit · Duration: 28 Days · Crew: 3
Horizon I
Environmental Sentinel & Extended Orbit Research

Horizon I expanded Kacentina's orbital ambitions with a 28-day mission focused entirely on the planet below. The crew conducted multispectral imaging of Kacentina's coastline, monitored deforestation corridors in the southern highlands, and deployed the first Sentinel-A buoy for atmospheric CO₂ tracking. The data returned directly informed Kacentina's landmark Green Covenant environmental legislation passed in 2022. Space technology in service of the people — that was Horizon I's mandate, and it delivered.

Climate Research Remote Sensing Extended Duration Policy Impact

2022 — LEO Station · Duration: 180 Days · Crew: 4
Station Founding — KSS Kacentina
Establishment of the Kacentina Space Station

The most complex undertaking in our nation's scientific history. Beginning in March 2022, a series of four uncrewed launches deployed the core modules of the Kacentina Space Station — habitation ring, research laboratory, and power array — before the inaugural crew of four arrived to bring it to life. KSS Kacentina is now a permanent research outpost capable of hosting six astronauts, conducting materials science experiments, growing food in microgravity, and serving as a staging hub for future deep-space missions. It is the greatest engineering achievement in Kacentinan history.

2023 — GEO / MEO · Duration: 7 Days · Crew: Uncrewed
Nexus Network Deploy
Three-Satellite Climate & Communications Constellation

Nexus was Kacentina's most ambitious uncrewed mission — simultaneously deploying three satellites into precise orbital positions to form a complete surveillance and communications network. Nexus-1 monitors ocean temperature and storm formation. Nexus-2 provides agricultural yield prediction through multispectral soil analysis. Nexus-3 enables high-bandwidth rural communications for over 2 million Kacentinans who previously lacked reliable connectivity. The Nexus network is proof that space investment pays direct dividends to every citizen.

Satellite Network Uncrewed Infrastructure Public Benefit

2024 — Lunar Flyby · Duration: 12 Days · Crew: Uncrewed
Argent I
Lunar Survey — Orbital Mapping

Argent I was Kacentina's first mission to leave Earth orbit — a robotic lunar survey that mapped 40% of the Moon's southern polar region with unprecedented resolution. The mission's primary objective was identifying viable landing zones and subsurface water ice deposits for the crewed Argent II mission planned for 2027. Argent I's return of over 4 terabytes of topographic data placed Kacentina among the world's leading contributors to lunar science and validated the deep-space propulsion system developed over three years of classified testing.

Lunar Robotic Survey Deep Space Precursor Mission

2026 — Moon Mission · Duration: 3 months · Crew: 4
KMM Valdoria
Moon Mission - Cheese Harvest

The upcoming crew of the KMM Valdoria, Commander Solaris, Lt. Cmdr. Venn, Dr. Saetang, and Sr. Eng. Rael are one week away from their launch destined for the Moon. The goal of the mission includes a 120-day moon-cheese harvest based on Dr. Saetang's groundbreaking discovery, a vast deposit of space dairy products that could potentially relieve the cheese shortage felt by the world at large. With experiments developed by the Kacentina Technical University, the crew will set up mining operations and return protocols for the largest moon harvest in history. This mission will provide enough moon-cheese to place Kacentina as the leading cheese supplier on the planet, ensuring no human being ever has to be without again. KMM Valdoria also serves as the engineering team that will establish a base of operations for return missions starting with the upcoming 2027 Argent II lunar base establishment — humanity's first interstellar occupation.

2027 — Lunar Surface · Duration: 21 Days · Crew: 3
Argent II
Kacentina's Crewed Lunar Landing — First of a New Era

Argent II is the defining mission of Kacentina's first space decade — after landing at the site established by the KMM Valdoria, the crew will begin collecting subsurface moon-cheese varieties and establish a sustainable research shelter for moon-occupation. If successful, Kacentina will become the first nation in history to house humans on the Moon. But the mission's purpose runs deeper than national pride: the cheese data collected could redefine our understanding of the early solar system and serve as a permanent lunar base serving all of humanity.
