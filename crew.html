A decorated pilot and aerospace engineer from Veloria, Tariq Solaris has spent 22 years in service to Kacentina's defense and scientific programs. Selected as KSA's first mission commander in 2021, Cmdr. Solaris has led three orbital missions and logged over 200 days in space. His calm authority and technical brilliance are credited with saving Rotation IV's crew during a critical pressure loss event aboard the station. Off-duty, he mentors youth through the NextGen Explorers initiative and holds a doctorate in orbital mechanics from the Kacentina Institute of Technology.

Brutus Yoke
Senior Agency Security — KSA

Lila Rael is the KSS's systems architect — the person responsible for keeping the station alive. Her expertise spans life support, power distribution, structural integrity, and thermal control. Rael joined KSA from the national aeronautics research division where she designed the next-generation solar array panels now powering the station. Colleagues describe her as "obsessively methodical," a trait she credits to her background in competitive rock climbing, where planning every move is the difference between summit and disaster. Currently on her third rotation aboard KSS.
