Commander Chedda McSwagga
Mission Commander — KMM Valdoria

Chedda McSwagga is a veteran KSA astronaut known for her intense leadership style, razor-sharp focus, and extremely high standards. Since joining the KSA Class of 2021, she has completed multiple space missions and earned a reputation for staying calm during high-pressure situations. Younger astronauts often credit her tough mentorship for preparing them for life in space. As commander of the KMM Valdoria mission, McSwagga oversees the entire crew and firmly believes there is exactly one correct way to organize a spacecraft—and everyone else is probably doing it wrong.

Lead Pilot — KMM Valdoria

Par Mesan is one of the KSA’s newest astronauts and quickly earned a reputation as an exceptionally talented pilot with remarkable reflexes under pressure. After joining the KSA Class of 2023, he completed his first orbital mission and proved himself during advanced flight and EVA operations. Unc Farquaad is one of the KSA’s most experienced Guidance and Navigation Specialists and has helped support missions for decades. Known around Mission Control as a walking encyclopedia of flight procedures, he has guided both crewed and unmanned launches with incredible reliability. Dr. Celeste Vantoon
Flight Director — KMM Valdoria

Dr. Celeste Vantoon is the KSA Flight Director responsible for overseeing all mission operations for the KMM Valdoria lunar mission. A highly respected aerospace specialist, she is known for her incredible focus, fast decision-making, and ability to manage complicated flight data under pressure. Animal Welfare Officer
Dr. Aura Sparkleton
Animal Welfare Officer — KSA

Dr. Aura Sparkleton is a senior Animal Welfare Officer and one of the KSA’s top experts in preparing animals for spaceflight. Since joining the KSA Class of 2019, she has helped train dozens of research animals for launch and zero-gravity environments while earning a reputation for her calm attitude and unconventional training methods. Sparkleton holds three doctoral degrees and is especially known for the strong bond she forms with the animals under her care, willing to do just about anything to keep them safe. For the KMM Valdoria mission, she is responsible for the health, safety, and training of Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom, the KSA’s first lunar research rat and possibly its most spoiled crew member.

Animal Pioneer
Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom
Animal Pioneer — KMM Valdoria

Remy Barnaby Bumblebottom is the KSA’s first rodent selected for a lunar mission and has quickly become one of the agency’s most popular crew members. Chosen for his impressive problem-solving skills and adaptability during zero-gravity testing, Remy has completed hundreds of maze trials and behavioral exercises under the care of Dr. Aura Sparkleton. While officially classified as a biological research payload, most KSA staff members treat him more like a tiny astronaut celebrity. During the KMM Valdoria mission, Remy will help researchers study how living organisms adapt to long-duration space travel—and hopefully continue his perfect record of locating hidden snacks.

Brutus Yoke is the longtime Head Security Officer for the Kacentina Space Agency and is known for taking security extremely seriously.
