Lt. Cmdr. Neva Venn
Lead Pilot — KSS Rotation VII

Neva Venn is widely considered one of the most naturally gifted pilots Kacentina has ever produced. After a decorated career in the Air Force where she broke the national altitude record in a modified interceptor, she transitioned to the KSA's inaugural pilot training program. Lt. Cmdr. She holds world records in manual orbital docking accuracy and is currently on her second long-duration mission aboard the Kacentina Space Station.

Mission Scientist
Dr. Emeka Okafor
Mission Scientist — Argent II

Dr. Okafor holds dual doctorates in astrophysics and xenogeology, and is the lead designer of Kacentina's lunar regolith sampling protocols. Born in the southern coastal city of Massara, he developed an obsession with the night sky that led him from a fishing village to the Kacentina Institute. His research on isotopic signatures in space dust has been cited in over 200 international papers. Currently in training for the ambitious Argent II lunar survey mission, Dr. Okafor describes his purpose simply: "To know what we are made of."

Engineers

Systems Engineer
Sr. Eng. Lila Rael
Senior Systems Engineer — KSS Rotation VII

Lila Rael is the KSS's systems architect — the person responsible for keeping the station alive. Her expertise spans life support, power distribution, structural integrity, and thermal control. Rael joined KSA from the national aeronautics research division where she designed the next-generation solar array panels now powering the station. Colleagues describe her as "obsessively methodical," a trait she credits to her background in competitive rock climbing, where planning every move is the difference between summit and disaster. Currently on her third rotation aboard KSS.

Flight Surgeons & Specialists

⚕️
Flight Surgeon
Dr. Jun Yune
Flight Surgeon — Ground Support, Veloria

Dr. Jun Yune is KSA's chief flight surgeon and the architect of the agency's microgravity medicine protocols, which have been adopted by allied space programs worldwide. A graduate of the Kacentina Medical Sciences University and former military trauma surgeon, Dr. Yune joined the space program after pioneering research into bone density preservation under extended low-gravity conditions. Though primarily ground-based, Dr. Yune is qualified for spaceflight and serves as the critical medical liaison for every KSS rotation, monitoring crew health in real time from Veloria.

Mission Specialist
Spec. Aria Caelum
Mission Specialist — Argent II Preparation

At 29, Aria Caelum is the youngest astronaut in the KSA program and one of its most versatile. Trained in remote sensing, robotics operations, and cartography, she is slated to serve as the primary operator of Argent II's autonomous surface rover — a machine she helped design. Caelum grew up in the high-altitude mountain city of Fenara, spending her childhood mapping trails and developing the spatial reasoning that would later make her one of the program's most promising specialists. Her motto: "The map is not the territory — go see it yourself."
